# Street Noise Recorder – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Street Noise Recorder is a free, open-source app for documenting street noise, such as cars revving
their engines, so residents can present evidence to their city. This policy explains what the app records
and where that data goes.

## What the app records

When you start recording, the app uses your device's microphone and camera to:

- **Measure the sound level** every second and keep a log of those levels.
- **Save a short video with sound** each time the noise rises well above the normal background level.
- **Note basic device health** (battery level, temperature, free storage, app version and device model)
  so you can check that your recorder is still working.

Recordings can capture whatever is within range of the microphone and camera, including passing voices,
people or vehicles.

## Where the data goes

- Recordings are stored **on your device** until they are uploaded.
- If you add a Hugging Face dataset and access token in Settings, the app uploads recordings and logs
  **to that dataset in your own Hugging Face account**, on the schedule you choose. Nothing is uploaded
  until you set this up.
- The app sends **no data to the developer** or to any other service. It contains no ads, analytics or
  tracking, and it has no developer server.
- Your Hugging Face token is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and is used only
  to upload to the dataset you chose.

Data you upload to Hugging Face is governed by Hugging Face's own terms and privacy policy, and by whether
you make your dataset public or private. Public datasets can be seen by anyone.

## Your responsibilities

You decide where the device is placed and what it records. Point the camera at public streets, not into
homes or private spaces, and follow your local laws. Some places restrict recording conversations or
people without their consent.

## Deleting data

- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it has stored on the device, including your token.
- Uploaded recordings can be deleted from your Hugging Face dataset at any time.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information about them.
